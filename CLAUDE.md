## Project

A Duolingo-style app for learning Romanian. Explanations are in Russian (`ru`) and Ukrainian (`ua`); every learner-facing string exists in both, enforced by the `Bi` type in `src/content/types.ts`. Interface copy lives in `src/i18n/ui.ts`.

- **Course content** is plain TypeScript data: `src/content/course.ts` is the map, one file per lesson under `src/content/lessons/`. The syllabus is `docs/curriculum-a1.md`, `docs/curriculum-a2.md`, `docs/curriculum-b1.md` and `docs/curriculum-b2.md`; units carry their `level`, the map numbers units within a level, and the A1 achievement counts only A1 lessons.
- **`src/engine/`** has no React in it: `check.ts` grades answers (normalization, diacritics-tolerant and one-typo-tolerant typed answers), `session.ts` is the lesson reducer (hearts, re-queueing wrong answers, progress, XP), `practice.ts` builds the "Тренировка" session from spaced-repetition state, `level.ts` and `achievements.ts` derive level and badges from progress (nothing about them is stored). Keep it that way so it stays easy to test.
- **Progress** is kept locally (`AsyncStorage`, `src/state/progress.tsx`) and, once signed in, mirrored to Supabase (`progress` table, one JSON row per user; schema in `supabase/schema.sql`). `src/state/model.ts` holds the pure parts: `merge()` takes the larger value of every field (streak, `lastDay` and freezes come together from the copy with the later `lastDay`; `recall` entries by the later `t`), so it is safe to run repeatedly and in any order — keep it that way. Settings (`lang`, `wordBank`, `dailyGoal`) stay per device and are not synced.
- **Start point and onboarding**: `progress.startAt` (a lesson id, synced, merged to the further one) opens that lesson and everything before it without marking them done; `isUnlocked(id, completed, startAt)` reads it. `onboarded` is per device. The first-run screen is `src/app/welcome.tsx`; the placement test is pure logic in `src/engine/placement.ts` (stages of two units, questions drawn from the course's pickNative/pickRo exercises). Progress saved before the alphabet unit existed is migrated to `startAt = 'u1l1'` in `progress.tsx`. The alphabet is unit `u0` with level `A0` and a `kicker` instead of a unit number.
- **Cards** (`src/content/cards.ts`): word-of-the-day, phrase and topic cards outside the course. `cardOfDay(date)` rotates words and phrases on the home screen; `/cards` has a search (headline, translation, examples and expressions, diacritics-insensitive) and a filter chip per group; it lists all (cards with a `theme` — work, docs, health, shop, transport, home, bank, food, social, travel, post, school — get their own sections first), `/card/[id]` shows one, and `card-<id>` in the lesson screen plays a practice built by `src/engine/cardPractice.ts` (not tracked for spaced repetition, counted as practice). Card phrases feed the phrase index after the course, and `scripts/check-glossary.ts` checks their words too.
- **Tap a word**: `TapText` (`src/components/PhraseSheet.tsx`) makes Romanian text tappable and opens one app-wide sheet (translation / words / explanation). Phrase translations come from the course itself (`src/engine/phrases.ts` indexes every exercise); word meanings from the hand-kept `src/content/glossary.ts`; readings from the lessons' own transcriptions, else from rules in `src/engine/transcribe.ts`. **New lessons must add their word forms to the glossary** — `npm run check:glossary` lists the gaps. Pass `hint` on prompts the learner still has to translate, so the sheet does not give the answer away. The sheet's `WalletButton` saves words to `progress.wallet` (keyed by the Romanian text, removals kept as `on: false` so the later change wins on sync); the dictionary screen's Wallet tab lists them. `src/engine/walletPractice.ts` turns them into a session at `/lesson/wallet` (meanings from the glossary's first sense, distractors from other saved words, then the glossary); its exercises carry empty keys, which `recordAnswer` skips, so they stay out of spaced repetition.
- **Daily goal and streak**: XP is counted per local day in `xpByDay`; the streak grows on the day the goal is first reached, not on any lesson. Missed days are covered by freezes (one per 7 streak days, at most `MAX_FREEZES`). **Spaced repetition**: every graded answer calls `recordAnswer("lessonId#index", ok)`, which moves the exercise between Leitner boxes (`INTERVALS`). Exercise keys are positional, so reordering exercises inside a finished lesson reshuffles its review history — append instead.
- **Auth** (`src/state/auth.tsx`) is email + password. `src/lib/supabase.ts` exports `null` when `EXPO_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL` / `EXPO_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY` are missing; every account feature must keep working as guest mode in that case. Account deletion (`delete_my_account` RPC) is required by the App Store — do not remove it.
- `EXPO_PUBLIC_*` values are inlined at build time; after changing them locally, export with `--clear` or Metro serves the old bundle.
- Speech is `expo-speech` with `ro-RO`; quality depends on the device's installed voices.

Package installs in this environment need `EXPO_OFFLINE=1 npx expo install <pkg>` (the Expo API is not reachable). `react` and `react-dom` must stay on the same exact version.
